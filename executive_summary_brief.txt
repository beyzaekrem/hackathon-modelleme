ECOSYSTEM WATER RESILIENCE: EXECUTIVE BRIEF
For Senior Decision-Makers

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY FINDINGS

• 324 protected ecosystems analyzed (17,383 km² total area)
• 20% high-risk, 46% medium-risk, 34% low-risk
• High-risk ecosystems represent 76% of total protected area (13,221 km²)
• 100% of high-risk ecosystems depend on sensitive groundwater aquifers
• Southeast region: 58.8% high-risk (CRITICAL PRIORITY)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY RISKS

1. WIDESPREAD WATER STRESS
   • Two-thirds of protected ecosystems face moderate to high water stress
   • Systemic challenge requiring coordinated national response

2. CRITICAL GROUNDWATER DEPENDENCY
   • 100% of high-risk ecosystems rely on sensitive aquifers
   • Vulnerable to over-extraction and contamination
   • Requires immediate protection measures

3. ELEVATED DROUGHT EXPOSURE
   • High-risk ecosystems: 35% higher drought exposure than average
   • Climate change may double high-risk ecosystems within decades
   • Requires immediate climate adaptation

4. DISPROPORTIONATE IMPACT ON LARGE AREAS
   • High-risk ecosystems: 20% by count, 76% by area
   • Most ecologically significant areas face greatest challenges

STRATEGIC ENVIRONMENTAL THREATS

1. CLIMATE CHANGE: Increasing drought frequency and intensity
   • Current: Moderate-high drought exposure (0.654 mean)
   • Projected: May double high-risk ecosystems within 2-3 decades
   • Mitigation: Climate adaptation planning, drought contingency, early warning

2. GROUNDWATER OVER-EXTRACTION: Threatening ecosystem water supply
   • Current: 100% of high-risk ecosystems depend on sensitive aquifers
   • Risk: Agricultural, urban, industrial water demand
   • Mitigation: Protection zones, extraction limits, basin-scale management

3. SOUTHEAST REGIONAL STRESS: Dual-stressor concentration
   • Current: 58.8% high-risk (vs. 20.1% national average)
   • Risk: Regional climate patterns + sensitive aquifers
   • Mitigation: Regional water security strategy, priority allocation

4. SYSTEMIC MODERATE RISK: Widespread vulnerability
   • Current: 46.3% medium-risk (150 ecosystems)
   • Risk: Gradual degradation, transition to high-risk
   • Mitigation: Preventive resilience-building, early intervention
