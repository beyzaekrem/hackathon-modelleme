ECOSYSTEM WATER RESILIENCE MODEL: RELIABILITY ASSESSMENT SUMMARY
====================================================================

OVERALL RELIABILITY RATING: ⚠️ MODERATE

Suitable for: Relative ranking, spatial pattern identification
Not suitable for: Absolute risk quantification, threshold-based decisions

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KEY DATA LIMITATIONS

1. WETLAND PROXIMITY RISK: 93.5% ZERO VALUES
   • Only 21 ecosystems (6.5%) have non-zero wetland proximity risk
   • Despite 20% weight, contributes only 1.3% to final scores
   • Indicates major data coverage gap or methodological limitation
   Impact: HIGH - Component effectively non-functional

2. DROUGHT EXPOSURE: 9.9% ZERO VALUES
   • 32 ecosystems show zero drought exposure
   • May indicate data gaps or spatial coverage issues
   • 50km buffer radius arbitrary, not validated
   Impact: MODERATE - Affects 48.7% of final scores

3. GROUNDWATER SENSITIVITY: COARSE CLASSIFICATION
   • Only 4 unique normalized values (0.0, 0.33, 0.67, 1.0)
   • Ordinal mapping (0.2, 0.5, 0.8) not validated
   • Missing continuous metrics (recharge, extraction pressure)
   Impact: HIGH - Affects 48.9% of final scores

4. PROTECTED AREA IMPORTANCE: MINIMAL VARIATION
   • Mean importance: 0.036 (very low)
   • Contributes only 1.1% to final scores despite 15% weight
   • Based only on area and park type, missing biodiversity factors
   Impact: LOW - Minimal contribution to final scores

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

POTENTIAL BIAS

1. WEIGHTING SCHEME BIAS: UNVALIDATED WEIGHTS
   • Intended weights differ significantly from actual contributions:
     - Drought: 48.7% actual vs. 35% intended
     - Groundwater: 48.9% actual vs. 30% intended
     - Wetland: 1.3% actual vs. 20% intended
     - Importance: 1.1% actual vs. 15% intended
   • Weights not empirically validated against observed impacts
   Impact: HIGH - Structural bias toward drought and groundwater

2. AGGREGATION BIAS: MAXIMUM OVERESTIMATION
   • Uses maximum value for overlapping zones
   • Assumes worst-case scenario, potentially overestimating risk
   • Ignores spatial extent of overlap
   Impact: MODERATE - May overestimate risk for partially overlapping zones

3. NORMALIZATION BIAS: RELATIVE SCORING
   • Min-max normalization creates relative scores, not absolute risk
   • Scores depend on dataset composition
   • Cannot compare across different datasets or time periods
   Impact: MODERATE - Limits absolute risk interpretation

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

UNCERTAINTY SOURCES

1. MEASUREMENT UNCERTAINTY
   • SPI to severity conversion: (3 - SPI) / 6 not validated
   • Groundwater sensitivity mapping (0.2, 0.5, 0.8) assumes equal intervals
   Uncertainty Level: HIGH

2. SPATIAL UNCERTAINTY
   • 50km drought buffer radius arbitrary, not validated
   • 5km wetland proximity threshold arbitrary, excludes 93.5% of ecosystems
   • Multiple CRS transformations may introduce minor spatial errors
   Uncertainty Level: HIGH

3. MODEL STRUCTURE UNCERTAINTY
   • Only 4 components, may miss important factors (surface water, water quality, land use)
   • Linear additive model assumes no interactions or threshold effects
   • No uncertainty quantification or propagation
   Uncertainty Level: MODERATE-HIGH

4. TEMPORAL UNCERTAINTY
   • Single time period analysis, no trends or projections
   • No seasonal patterns or multi-year drought assessment
   Uncertainty Level: MODERATE

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RECOMMENDATIONS TO IMPROVE ACCURACY

PRIORITY 1: IMMEDIATE ACTIONS (0-6 months)
1. Expand wetland data coverage
   • Audit wetland dataset completeness
   • Expand proximity threshold or use distance-decay function
   • Expected Impact: HIGH

2. Validate and recalibrate weights
   • Collect observed impact data
   • Use regression/ML to identify optimal weights
   • Expected Impact: HIGH

3. Implement area-weighted aggregation
   • Replace maximum with area-weighted mean
   • Expected Impact: MODERATE

PRIORITY 2: SHORT-TERM ACTIONS (6-12 months)
4. Develop continuous groundwater metrics
   • Integrate recharge rates, extraction pressure
   • Replace ordinal classification
   • Expected Impact: HIGH

5. Multi-temporal drought analysis
   • Integrate multiple SPI time scales
   • Calculate drought frequency/duration
   • Expected Impact: MODERATE-HIGH

6. Develop absolute risk thresholds
   • Map observed impacts to model scores
   • Establish validated thresholds
   • Expected Impact: MODERATE-HIGH

PRIORITY 3: LONG-TERM ACTIONS (12+ months)
7. Independent validation
   • Collect observed impact data
   • Compare predictions to observations
   • Expected Impact: CRITICAL

8. Uncertainty quantification
   • Quantify input uncertainty
   • Use Monte Carlo simulation
   • Report confidence intervals
   • Expected Impact: HIGH

9. Model structure improvements
   • Add missing components (surface water, water quality, land use)
   • Test non-linear models
   • Expected Impact: MODERATE-HIGH

CONCLUSION

The model provides MODERATE RELIABILITY for relative vulnerability ranking but
contains significant limitations affecting absolute risk interpretation:

• Major data coverage gaps (93.5% zero wetland risk)
• Component imbalance (weights differ from actual contributions)
• Methodological uncertainties (arbitrary thresholds, unvalidated weights)
• No uncertainty quantification or independent validation

RECOMMENDATION: Use for relative ranking and spatial patterns, but NOT for
absolute risk quantification or threshold-based decisions without validation.

Next Steps: Implement Priority 1 recommendations (wetland data expansion, weight
validation, area-weighted aggregation) before using for critical decisions.
